Artisan Statement

I craft leather goods grounded in architectural durability, structural integrity, and uncompromising artisanal standards. My approach combines industrial machinery precision with meticulous bench detailing — from reinforced stress joints to dense, glass-smooth edge finishing.

Each piece is engineered for generational longevity: premium vegetable-tanned hides, supple lambskin, and bridle grades selected for aging character, assembled to maintain form and poise across decades of use.

Experience & Milestones

2021 — PRESENT

Founder & Master Craftsman • JG Leather

Officially registered independent artisan brand (NACE 1512). Complex pattern drafting from scratch, bespoke structural commissions, and small-batch production of luxury travel and lifestyle goods.

2022 — PRESENT

Upholstery & Ergonomic Seating Restoration

Professional training and execution of fine upholstery: structural restoration, leather tensioning, and bespoke re-trimming of ergonomic office seating and executive furniture.

2014 — PRESENT

Bespoke Leathercraft, Traditional Hand Stitching & Pattern Making

Over a decade of dedicated leathercraft practice: geometric pattern drafting from raw concept, traditional two-needle saddle stitching, and fine edge finishing. Expert integration of manual benchwork with specialized heavy-duty industrial machinery for structural durability.

2014 — 2023

Industrial Heavy Stitching & Marine Fabrication

Practical mastery of heavy-duty industrial sewing under extreme durability standards: manufacturing certified heavy lifting slings, custom canvas covers for lifeboats, fast rescue crafts (FRC), winches, and marine equipment. Confident handling of vast acreage of heavy canvas, heavy sailcloth, high-tensile webbing, and load-bearing hardware.
